I started my career as an engineer, deeply curious about how things work and driven to build efficient, scalable solutions. Over time, I found myself wanting to understand more than the how — I wanted to know the why.

Why are we building this? Who is it for? How do we know it's the right problem to solve? That curiosity led me to start joining product calls outside my core responsibilities. I'd listen in, ask questions, and study how PMs thought, communicated, and made decisions. It didn't take long before I realized this is what I wanted to do.

I rose to lead initiatives on products that serve hundreds of thousands of customers daily — at BlackRock, GRUBBRR, and Western Union. Today, I also mentor PMs as a coach on Leland, focused on visa candidates and non-traditional backgrounds — a path I walked myself.
